VIIIGovernance, Ethics & Risk

The proposal's ethics chapter is one of its stronger sections — the commitments are appropriate and specific. The problem is that none of them are implemented in the described pipeline, and two of them (X/Twitter and Reddit collection; username anonymisation) are incompatible with the free-tier data regime this project is now scoped to. This Part rewrites the commitments so that each one is true of the system that will be built, rather than aspirational.

8.1 Ethics and compliance commitments

PrincipleCommitmentHow it is enforced
Data provenanceAll data obtained through documented public APIs and RSS feeds, respecting each source's terms of service and rate limits.Every collector declares its source, licence and access method in config/sources.yaml and a data card. The rate limiter is configured per host from the source's published limits. No scraping of pages that prohibit it; no credential sharing.
Terms of serviceNo automated collection from sources whose terms prohibit it, and no collection of paywalled or restricted content.RSS feeds only — feeds are published for consumption. Articles behind a paywall are not fetched. A source is added only after a team member has read its terms and recorded the conclusion in the data card.
PrivacyNo personal data is collected, stored, modelled or reported. No usernames, no user identifiers, no profile data.Now structural rather than aspirational. The project collects only published news articles from institutional publications. There is no user-generated content in the corpus at all, so the anonymisation requirement is satisfied by construction rather than by a scrubbing step. If a social source is ever added, the ingestion layer must drop identifiers before persistence, enforced by a schema that has no field for them.
Bias & representativenessSentiment must not be a proxy for a single outlet's editorial policy.Multiple independent feeds (≥ 3) are collected. The sentiment layer reports a per-source distribution comparison (Table 4.1). The dashboard exposes the source mix. The thesis reports the bias check as a limitation.
Bot & manipulationNo dependence on content that could be coordinated or automated.A direct consequence of the news-only design. News is editorial, not crowdsourced, so it is structurally far less susceptible to coordinated posting than a social feed. Where a feed carries promotional or sponsored content, this is noted in the data card as a residual limitation.
Market-update exclusionArticles that merely recap past price moves are excluded from the sentiment corpus.Implemented in the sentiment layer, following Kreuzer et al. (2026). This is both an ethics measure (avoid circular reasoning about price driving news) and a methodological necessity — it addresses endogeneity, and without it the sentiment-return relationship is partly self-referential.
No investment adviceThe output is academic research. It is not a trading system, not a recommendation, and not advice.Enforced in the artefacts: every model card carries an out-of-scope section; the dashboard footer states it on every page; the API returns a disclaimer field; the thesis concludes with it. No order routing exists in the codebase, so the boundary is enforced by architecture, not only by prose.
TransparencyMethodology, data sources, feature definitions, model architecture and limitations are documented and reproducible.The documentation set of Part VII, the run manifests, the data cards and the model cards. A third party can re-run every number.
No overstatementResults are reported at their true strength, including non-significant and contradictory findings.The experiment log records negative results as first-class entries. The thesis reports the CI on every effect estimate. The model cards list known failure modes explicitly, including where the model should not be trusted.
Attribution of prior workPublished methods, datasets and figures are used with credit, and any adaptation is marked as such.The reimplementation of Dashtaki et al. (2025)'s architecture is cited as such in the code, the thesis and the model card. The sentiment methodology is cited to Kreuzer et al. (2026) in the module docstring. Adapted figures carry an attribution note.

Why the news-only design is ethically cleaner, not merely cheaper

The original proposal's social-media-based design would have required bot detection, coordinated-posting detection, per-user fairness analysis, and a genuine personal-data regime. The news-only design eliminates all four — not by neglecting them, but by scoping the construct to a channel where they do not apply. This is worth stating explicitly in the thesis's ethics chapter, because it converts a limitation into a considered design decision. The framing is: we measure the rationally-priced news channel, which prior work identifies as the channel institutions actually respond to, and we therefore avoid the manipulation and privacy problems of social sentiment entirely.
