The four questions you will be asked — and the short answers

"Why not just XGBoost?" — "XGBoost is our primary model. Two published studies found tree ensembles outperform deep sequence models on crypto returns, so we made the tree the primary and the LSTM the benchmark. Our contribution is not the model; it is what the model reveals about how sentiment behaves differently across asset tiers."

"How do you know you have no leakage?" — "We have an automated causality test that recomputes every feature at time t using only data up to t and asserts the values are identical. It runs in CI. We also embargo every split boundary by 61 days and fit the scaler only on training folds. The test is in the repository."

"Why is your accuracy barely above 50%?" — "Because that is the honest number for this problem, and we report balanced accuracy and MCC alongside it because raw accuracy near 50% would be misleading. Published accuracy figures on this task in the 97% range are not comparable to ours because they use a different target, a different protocol and no embargo. Our random-walk baseline is the reference, and our improvement over it is measured out-of-sample across 38 walk-forward folds."

"Is this investment advice?" — "No. It is a research artefact. The system has no order-routing capability by design, every model card states its out-of-scope uses, and the thesis states the boundary explicitly."
